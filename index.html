VINYL?

I feel like listening to music has become so easy because we can just open an app and play whatever we want. Maybe because of this people don't see the value in collecting physical media as much nowadays or they don't think vinyl offers a different listening experience. That's my main question I'm going to explore. IS there really a difference or are people just collecting vinyl for the novelty of it? I have a bunch of records that I listen to all the time. But I've never actually sat down and compared listening to the same music on vinyl and Spotify side by side. For me part of the appeal is appreciating physical media and keeping it alive but I also want to go beyond my own perspective and see if there's actually a noticeable difference in the experience. With these three album reviews I want to figure out what changes are between the two formats (if anything) and whether the way we listen to music affects how we appreciate it.
